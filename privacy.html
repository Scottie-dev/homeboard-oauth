HomeBoard privacy policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

Purpose

HomeBoard is a private, non-commercial home dashboard used by one household. It is not offered to the public.

Google Calendar data

HomeBoard requests read-only access to Google Calendar. It uses that access to:

  • list calendars selected by the owner;
  • read event titles, dates, times and event colours; and
  • display those events on the owner's HomeBoard screen.

HomeBoard does not create, edit or delete Google Calendar events.

Storage and sharing

Calendar data and OAuth credentials are stored only on the owner's HomeBoard device. Credentials are encrypted using a separate local key. Google user data is not sold, used for advertising, or shared with third parties.

Retention and removal

Cached calendar data is refreshed for the display and remains on the owner's device. The owner can disconnect Google Calendar from HomeBoard and remove its locally stored credentials at any time. Access can also be revoked from the owner's Google Account permissions.

Limited use

HomeBoard's use of information received from Google APIs complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
